Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

Vui Mỗi Ngày ("the App") is a personal desktop tool operated by Tidus. This policy explains what data the App accesses when you connect your TikTok account.

2. Data we access

  • Basic profile info (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Publishing permission (scope video.publish): used only to upload videos that you choose to publish to your own account.
  • OAuth tokens issued by TikTok, required to call the API on your behalf.

3. How data is stored

All data, including access tokens and video files, is stored locally on the owner's computer. The App has no server, does not run analytics, and does not collect data from other TikTok users.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok's official API to perform the actions you request. Subtitle text may be processed by a translation service solely to generate subtitles; no TikTok account data is included.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you revoke access or delete them. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok account settings (Security > Manage app permissions); the App's stored tokens then stop working and can be deleted locally.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes take effect when published on this page.
